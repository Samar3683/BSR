## Generation direction

Standalone photorealistic 16:9 backgrounds, consistent white BSR trucks, graphite BSR lettering, green–white–red livery, cinematic Italian environments, no website UI or headlines. Scene-specific compositions reserve space for left, right or centered text. Prompt requests were for 3840 × 2160; actual returned dimensions are recorded above.
